Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Who I am

"My Claude Assistant" (the "App") is a personal tool operated by Jackson Wilhard. This policy explains what information the App accesses and how it is used.

2. Information the App accesses

When you authorize the App through TikTok, it may access, depending on the permissions you approve:

  • Basic profile information (display name, avatar, username, bio).
  • Account statistics (follower count, following count, likes count, video count).
  • Your public videos and their engagement figures (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • The ability to publish videos or photos to your account when you ask it to.

The App does not access your TikTok password. Login happens on TikTok's own pages.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to show you your own account statistics and videos and to publish content you have asked to publish. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Sharing and selling

I do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Information may pass through the service providers needed to run the App (such as the integration platform that handles the TikTok connection), only for the purpose of providing the features described above.

5. Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored by the integration platform only for as long as the connection is active. I do not keep a separate database of your TikTok data.

6. Your choices

You can revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok account settings, and you can ask me to delete any data related to you by emailing the address below.

7. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your region).

8. Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
